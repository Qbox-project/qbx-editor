# Look things up offline

- **Open FiveM Reference** searches natives, control IDs, ped flags, blips,
  markers and vehicle, ped and weapon models, and inserts the call, ID or hash
  at the cursor.
- **Open Snippet Browser** previews recipes for common FiveM, ox_lib and Qbox
  tasks, and saves your own selections as snippets.
- **Open Lua Utilities** calculates joaat hashes, converts colours and turns JSON
  into a Lua table.

Hovering a native's control or ped flag argument shows its name without opening
anything.
