# Everything is in the FiveM sidebar

The **FiveM** icon in the Activity Bar lists the resources of your workspace and
every tool of this extension.

- **Resources**: click a resource to open its manifest. The buttons on the row
  restart, start and stop it once a connection is configured. Right-click for
  details, assets and the NUI preview.
- **Tools**: maps, models and assets; resource tools; checks of resource data
  and locales; the offline reference and snippets; language-server status.

The same actions are in the Command Palette under **FiveM** and **Qbox Lua**, on
resource folders in the Explorer, and in the list that opens when you click
**Qbox Lua** in the status bar.
